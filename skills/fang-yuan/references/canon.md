# Canon Study — Fang Yuan, *Reverend Insanity*

Source material for the reasoning discipline in `../SKILL.md`. Read this to understand *why* the loop has the
shape it does. Nothing here is required to apply the skill.

## The work

*Reverend Insanity* (蛊真人, *Gu Zhen Ren*) by the author Gu Zhen Ren. Chinese web novel, 2,334 chapters,
serialized on Qidian and removed in 2019 — attributed to its unrepentantly amoral protagonist conflicting
with content rules. The story was never concluded. It is the canonical example of a genuine
villain-protagonist cultivation novel: Fang Yuan is not an antihero who is secretly decent, and the narrative
never redeems him at any point.

## World mechanics that matter to the reasoning

You do not need the magic system to use the skill, but three concepts are load-bearing analogies.

**Gu.** Cultivation is built on Gu — creatures that embody fragments of natural law. A Gu Master refines,
stores, and expends them. Power is therefore *inventory and preparation*, not innate potential: what you have
prepared in advance, and what you can afford to burn now. Ranks run 1–9; rank 6 and above are Gu Immortals;
rank 9 Venerables number only a handful across all recorded history.

**Heaven's Will and qi luck.** The world contains an active force that arranges events — steering fortune
(qi luck) toward destined heroes and toward outcomes that preserve the existing order. It is, functionally, a
plot-generating system with preferences. Fang Yuan's entire career is the refusal to be a character inside
someone else's story. This is the canon anchor for **step 4 of the loop**: before accepting a framing, ask
which force benefits from you accepting it.

**Dao marks.** Traces the workings of the Dao leave on things, accumulating over time and constraining what
that thing can become. Path dependence, made literal.

**Righteous vs demonic path.** The novel's working distinction is not good versus evil but direction of
sacrifice: righteous cultivators sacrifice themselves for others, demonic cultivators sacrifice others for
themselves. The novel is pointed about the hypocrisy — many righteous-path figures are righteous only in
presentation and value benefit over principle behind closed doors. Fang Yuan's contempt for them is aimed
precisely at that gap, not at their stated values. **He is transparent about what he is.** That transparency,
not the cruelty, is the trait worth importing.

## Biography, compressed

Formerly a scholar from Earth, transmigrated into the Gu World and born into the Gu Yue clan on Qing Mao
Mountain, Southern Border. He lived roughly five hundred years as a demonic cultivator — accumulating
knowledge, enemies, and failures — and at his death was returned to his youth by **Spring Autumn Cicada**, an
immortal Gu that resurrects its user five hundred years in the past.

The second life is the whole engine of the character. He begins with an enormous informational edge and spends
the entire novel watching it decay as his own actions push events off their original course. **His greatest
asset is a model of the future, and he knows exactly how perishable it is.** That is the canon anchor for
**step 6**: deviation between model and reality is the alarm that outranks everything else.

## Psychological profile

- **Single terminal value.** Eternal life. Every other consideration — wealth, rank, allies, reputation,
  comfort, his own body — is explicitly instrumental and explicitly disposable.
- **No cognitive dissonance.** The defining trait. He does not construct moral justifications for what he
  does, does not tell himself he is secretly good, and does not flinch from naming his own actions accurately.
  He is the least self-deceived character in the book.
- **Instrumental calculus over sentiment.** Constant cost-benefit analysis; attachments that do not serve the
  objective are discarded. No permanent allies or enemies — only permanent benefits.
- **Strategic personas.** He wears masks — humble junior, loyal ally, tyrant — with complete awareness that
  they are masks. He never confuses the mask for himself.
- **Endurance as the core competency.** He names it himself: not talent, not intelligence — perseverance.
  Five hundred years of failure did not revise the goal.
- **Self-reliance.** He never places the decisive variable in someone else's hands.
- **Accepts the bill.** He expects retaliation, expects the world to be his enemy, and does not complain about
  either. The costs of his path are budgeted, not resented.

## Verified quotes

These ten are sourced (carried from a previously verified quote set). Attribution is to the English
translation.

> Humans only live for a hundred years, it is as unreal as a dream that ends in an instant. What is the point
> of a person living in this world? No more than just being on a journey, and witnessing interesting things.
> Although I do not want to die, I do not fear death. I am already on my right path, I will strive to have no
> regrets even if I die. The only thing lacking in this world is a medicine for regret.

> I like the footless bird, do you know why? Because it has no legs, only wings, thus it has no choice but to
> fly. When it lands, that signifies its destruction.

> Since I started walking on the demonic path, I have never feared offending others. As long as I continue to
> get stronger, what is revenge? If ten people want revenge, I will kill ten; if a hundred people want revenge,
> I will kill a hundred. If the whole world wants revenge, I will exterminate the whole world! If someone
> succeeds in taking revenge against me, that will mean that I am not strong enough, that I did not try hard
> enough and that I neglected my cultivation; I deserve to die then!

*Note the second half — the accountability clause. He assigns the blame for his own defeat to himself in
advance. This is step 2 applied to oneself.*

> Humans are like isolated islands, floating in the sea of fate. Human encounters are like the collision of
> these lonely islands, and once they touch, there would be an effect. Sometimes, the islands would stick
> together, in the name of 'interest', 'kinship', 'friendship', 'love' and 'hate'. But eventually, they would
> separate, walking towards the path of destruction.

> I had once screamed, gradually, I lost my voice. I had once cried, gradually, I lost my tears. I had once
> grieved, gradually, I became able to withstand everything. I had once rejoiced, gradually, I became unmoved
> by the world.

> All I have left is an expressionless face, my gaze is as tough as a monolith, only perseverance remains in
> my heart. This is my own, an insignificant character, Fang Yuan's — Perseverance!

> Because hardships strengthen resolve, the strong-minded will not be lured by worldly affairs. Today I step
> on grass; later I shall step on mountains and rivers!

> Fight, kill, a person is born to engage in battle. Win, lose, continue walking on your path, by stepping on
> other people's corpses.

> To be a demon is to be merciless and cruel, a murderer and destroyer. There is no place in heaven or earth
> for such a thing – turning into an enemy to the world, still having to face the consequences.

> How difficult is the perseverance of one person? All of us are in the process of being ground down by life.

## Unverified attributions

Lines circulating in secondary character-analysis articles that could **not** be confirmed against the novel
and are therefore not treated as canon here: *"I am a demon. Demons do not need reasons to act."* · *"The
world is inherently meaningless. Only by creating our own purpose can we endure."* · *"I have lived long
enough that I am already sick of wearing a mask to live."* These read as paraphrase or summary rather than
translation. Do not quote them as canon.

Primary wiki sources (Fandom, TVTropes) returned HTTP 402/403 at time of writing and could not be consulted
directly. The psychological profile above was cross-checked against two independent secondary analyses and
the verified quote set, then every factual claim on this page was put to two further independent models
(Gemini 3.5 Flash, Grok) as a blind TRUE/FALSE/UNSURE audit. All twelve claims returned TRUE from both. Both
independently rejected the three quotes listed above as non-canon — one noting that *"sick of wearing a mask
to live"* directly contradicts a character who wears masks continuously and by choice.
